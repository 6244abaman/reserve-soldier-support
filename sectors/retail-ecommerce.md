# Retail and e-commerce

> Shared rule. Sector files give context and questions, not facts about Israeli law. Licensing, tax, and grant rules come only from the maintained reference document or an official source.

Covers shops, online stores, market sellers. Money tied up in stock, seasonal peaks, supplier terms.

- **Ask:** how much stock is unsold and how old; best-margin products; online share of sales.
- **Common problems:** dead stock, missed seasons, cold ad accounts, late supplier payments.
- **Pricing:** margin per product; clear old stock to free cash.
- **Customers:** past-buyer lists, social media, marketplaces, Google Business profile.
- **Watch:** return policies and consumer rules, confirmed with an official source.
