# Tech and creative

> Shared rule. Sector files give context and questions, not facts about Israeli law. Licensing, tax, and grant rules come only from the maintained reference document or an official source.

Covers developers, designers, photographers, video, marketing creatives. Irregular project and retainer income, with late payment common.

- **Ask:** current pipeline; past clients who could rehire; real hourly earnings on a typical project.
- **Common problems:** outdated portfolio, lost clients, underpricing, growing scope.
- **Pricing:** by value and scope, limited revisions, and a deposit.
- **Customers:** portfolio refresh, past clients, online communities, a few clear packages.
- **Watch:** contracts and intellectual property terms, reviewed by a professional for larger jobs.
