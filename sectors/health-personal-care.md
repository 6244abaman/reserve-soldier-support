# Health and personal care

> Shared rule. Sector files give context and questions, not facts about Israeli law. Licensing, tax, and grant rules come only from the maintained reference document or an official source.

Covers therapists, trainers, clinics, hair and beauty, wellness studios. Appointment-based income, fixed rent, repeat clients.

- **Ask:** appointments per week now versus before; cancellation rate; share of regulars.
- **Common problems:** clients moved to others, empty calendar, idle space costing rent.
- **Pricing:** per session, packages, or memberships, and the cost of an empty slot.
- **Customers:** past-client messages, referrals, a booking link, local groups.
- **Watch:** professional licenses and confidentiality, confirmed with the relevant body. Do not give medical advice.
