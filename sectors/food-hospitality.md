# Food and hospitality

> Shared rule. Sector files give context and questions, not facts about Israeli law. Licensing, tax, and grant rules come only from the maintained reference document or an official source.

Covers restaurants, cafes, catering, food trucks, home bakers. Thin margins, daily cash, high fixed costs, spoilage risk.

- **Ask:** food cost and labor cost as a share of sales; five best sellers versus most profitable; who works shifts when away.
- **Common problems:** lost regulars, staff who left, tightened supplier credit, outdated menu prices.
- **Pricing:** per dish from ingredient cost plus labor; cut low-margin items before raising prices.
- **Customers:** local groups, delivery apps, event catering, WhatsApp orders.
- **Watch:** health and business license deadlines, confirmed in the reference document.
