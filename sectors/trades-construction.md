# Trades and construction

> Shared rule. Sector files give context and questions, not facts about Israeli law. Licensing, tax, and grant rules come only from the maintained reference document or an official source.

Covers electricians, plumbers, carpenters, contractors, renovation. Project-based income, materials paid up front, late customer payments.

- **Ask:** jobs booked now and next month; how quotes are built and how often accepted; late-paying customers.
- **Common problems:** empty pipeline after months away, underpriced old quotes, unpaid invoices, missing subcontractors.
- **Pricing:** hours, materials, travel and a margin for surprises, with a clear deposit.
- **Customers:** referrals, local groups, reviews, photos of finished work.
- **Watch:** licenses, insurance, and safety requirements, confirmed in the reference document.
