# Transport and logistics

> Shared rule. Sector files give context and questions, not facts about Israeli law. Licensing, tax, and grant rules come only from the maintained reference document or an official source.

Covers drivers, delivery, moving, small fleets, courier work. Heavy vehicle costs, fuel, insurance, income tied to hours on the road.

- **Ask:** fixed monthly vehicle costs; real earnings per route or job after fuel and time; customers who give steady work.
- **Common problems:** lost contracts, idle vehicles, insurance and maintenance falling behind.
- **Pricing:** cost per kilometer and per hour, plus empty return trips, with a minimum charge.
- **Customers:** business accounts, regular routes, partnerships with local shops, referrals.
- **Watch:** vehicle licensing and insurance deadlines, confirmed in the reference document.
