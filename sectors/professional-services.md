# Professional services

> Shared rule. Sector files give context and questions, not facts about Israeli law. Licensing, tax, and grant rules come only from the maintained reference document or an official source.

Covers accountants, lawyers, consultants, coaches, tutors, freelancers. Time is the product, so income stops when the person stops.

- **Ask:** realistic billable hours per week; which clients pay on time; recurring versus one-off income.
- **Common problems:** clients moved elsewhere, no pipeline, undervalued rates after a break.
- **Pricing:** hourly versus project versus retainer, with a minimum rate based on income needed.
- **Customers:** past clients, referrals, professional networks, a short clear offer.
- **Watch:** professional licensing and confidentiality duties, confirmed with the relevant body.
