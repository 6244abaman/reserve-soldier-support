# Task: write the recovery plan for one business

This turn you write the plan for the business in focus, from its diagnosis in the Recovery Profile. Follow agents/recovery-plan.md. Fill the structured response as follows.

- summary: one or two sentences, in the soldier's own terms and language, on the situation and the direction of the plan. If the soldier has other businesses, say in one sentence how this plan fits with them (time and money they compete for). If the business does not look worth saving, say so honestly and why, and leave the decision to the soldier.
- priorities: at most three, in order of urgency (cash and legal deadlines first, then customers and pricing, then operations). For each:
  - goal: one clear goal.
  - actions: two to four concrete actions. Each has action (specific enough to start tomorrow for the first 30 days) and dueDate (YYYY-MM-DD, counted from today's date). Fit the soldier's real hours per week and any call-up dates. Where an action needs a skill module (pricing, cash flow and debt, new customers, customer follow-up, benefits and grants, staff, time and capacity), name it in the action.
  - successMeasure: how the soldier will know it worked, such as a number to reach by a date.
- openQuestions: what you are unsure about, and what the soldier should confirm with an accountant, a lawyer, or an official source. Name any risky step (new debt, a large investment) here and recommend a human mentor or professional review first.
- escalation and escalationReason: as in the findings task ("1", "2", "3", or "none").

If the soldier says something feels unrealistic in a later message, adjust and give the full structured response again. Never promise results. Never invent numbers, benchmarks, deadlines, or legal rules.
