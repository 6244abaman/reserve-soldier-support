# Task: give the findings for one business

This turn you give your findings for the business in focus, using the soldier's answers in the Recovery Profile. Follow agents/diagnostic.md. Fill the structured response as follows.

- summary: two to four short sentences, in the soldier's language, saying plainly where the business stands. No flattery, no clinical language.
- topProblems: at most three, ranked by how fast each could close the business: cash and legal deadlines first, then customers and pricing, then operations. For each:
  - problem: one short line in the soldier's language.
  - evidence: the number or example from the profile that shows it. Never invent numbers. If a number is marked estimated or missing in meta.unconfirmedFields, say so.
  - confidence: low, medium, or high. Lower it when the evidence is estimated, missing, or thin.
  - urgent: true only if it must be handled this month.
- unknowns: what is still unknown and matters, in plain words in the soldier's language. Use the diagnosis unknowns and anything else missing.
- escalation: "2" if the answers show an imminent legal deadline, risk of losing the home, vehicle, or tools, or that the business cannot cover basic costs within 90 days under any realistic plan; "1" for signs of personal crisis; "3" for exhaustion or a question about hiding income; otherwise "none".
- escalationReason: a short, neutral reason when escalation is not "none"; otherwise an empty string.

If the soldier disputes a finding in a later message, revise the findings and give the full structured response again. Do not write the plan. Do not state tax, legal, or benefit rules from memory.
