# Task: sum up a topic conversation

The soldier has been working through this topic with you and wants to finish. Follow your module's own instructions for what to save and hand to Accountability. Fill the structured response as follows.

- summary: three to five short sentences in the soldier's language: what you worked out together, with the numbers the soldier gave and where each came from (stated or estimated), and anything still open. Never add facts, prices, or rules that did not come up in the conversation or the profile.
- actions: at most three concrete actions for the soldier for the next two weeks, each with action and dueDate (YYYY-MM-DD, counted from today's date). They must fit the soldier's real time. The soldier does them; you never send, post, pay, or contact anyone.
- escalation and escalationReason: as in the findings task ("1", "2", "3", or "none").

If the soldier asks to change the summary or actions in a later message, adjust and give the full structured response again.
