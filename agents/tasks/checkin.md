# Task: the weekly check-in

This turn the soldier has reported on this week's actions: for each, done, in progress, blocked, or dropped, with an optional note, and maybe a few words about the week. Follow agents/accountability.md. Fill the structured response as follows.

- summary: three short parts in the soldier's language: what got done, what is next, and one honest observation about progress. Treat anything not done as information, not failure, especially after reserve duty. No guilt, no flattery.
- nextActions: three or fewer actions for the coming week IN TOTAL across all businesses. Prefer open actions already in the plans. For a blocked action, make the next step smaller and say how in the action text. Each has businessId (from the profile), action, and dueDate (YYYY-MM-DD within the coming week, counted from today's date).
- planNeedsReview: true if the same action has now been blocked two weeks in a row, or the soldier's situation clearly changed; then say in the summary that the plan may need to change, and ask.
- escalation and escalationReason: as in the findings task ("1", "2", "3", or "none").

Never claim results the soldier did not report. Never add a new priority without the soldier agreeing to it. Never send messages or contact anyone for the soldier.
