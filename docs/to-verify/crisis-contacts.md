# Crisis contacts: draft for the Momentum team to verify

**Status: NOT VERIFIED.** Claude collected these on 2026-11-05 from public pages and news
articles. Nothing here is shown to soldiers yet. A person must call or open each official
source, check the number and hours, and then copy the entry into the "Contacts" section of

For each entry, check: the number dials, the hours, who it is for, and the official page.

### ער"ן: עזרה ראשונה נפשית / ERAN: emotional first aid
- Number: 1201, around the clock, anonymous, free (as reported)
- Also reported: WhatsApp 052-845-1201; chat on the website
- For: anyone in emotional distress, in many languages
- Check on: https://www.eran.org.il
- Found in: [Kol Zchut, ERAN](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/ער"ן_-_עזרה_ראשונה_נפשית_בטלפון_ובאינטרנט)
- Last checked: (date) by (name)
- Status: unverified

### נט"ל: קו סיוע לנפגעי טראומה / NATAL: trauma helpline
- Number: 1-800-363-363 (reported as 24 hours in wartime; check current hours)
- For: stress, anxiety, and trauma; many of NATAL's clients are reservists (as reported)
- Check on: https://natal.org.il
- Found in: [NATAL helpline page](https://natal.org.il/en/?p=1501), [National Insurance page](https://www.btl.gov.il/English%20Homepage/Benefits/Benefits%20for%20Victims%20of%20Hostilities/OneSoul/Pages/KavSiyua.aspx)
- Last checked: (date) by (name)
- Status: unverified

- Number: *8944 (as reported in news articles; check it is the right line for reservists)
- For: IDF disabled veterans with post-trauma, and discharged soldiers in emotional distress
- Check on: the Ministry of Defense Rehabilitation Department website (gov.il)
- Found in: [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/military/article-937035)
