# Israeli benefits reference

This file is the only source the Benefits and grants module and the Cash flow and debt module may use for rules, amounts, and deadlines. It starts as a template with no figures, because rules change and nothing here has been verified yet. Each entry is filled in by a person who opens the official source and records what it says.

> **Open task.** Someone on the Momentum team should fill the first entries from official sources before the benefits agent is shown to real soldiers. Until then, the module's answer to every specific question is: "I can't confirm the current rules. Here is who can."

## Rules for this file
- An entry counts as verified only if it has a source link to an official page, the date it was checked, and the name of the person who checked it.
- Each entry has a review-by date. After that date the agents treat it as stale and say so.
- Amounts, percentages, and deadlines are copied exactly from the source, with the page they came from. No rounding, no paraphrased figures.
- Entries the team could not verify stay marked "unverified" and the agents point to the human mentor instead.
- Hebrew is the source language; add an English summary beside it.

## Entry template (copy for each program)

```markdown
### [Program name in Hebrew and English]
- Category: reserve-duty compensation | business support | tax or payment relief | loan or guarantee | municipal | counseling
```
